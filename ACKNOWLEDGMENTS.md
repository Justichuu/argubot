# Acknowledgments

argubot is directed and published by Justichuu.

The program is being shaped through human direction and AI assistance. The
human sets the joke, the fairness rule, the publication boundary, and the
final yes or no. AI tools help with implementation, tests, wording, and
review. That split is copied from
[Private Directory Server](https://github.com/Justichuu/private-directory-server),
which refuses to treat "vibe coding" as an excuse for unverifiable software.

Contributions are accepted based on mirrored pairs, passing tests, and a
record somebody can review. They are not accepted or rejected based on
whether the first draft came from a person or an AI system.

Thanks also go to anyone who adds a pair, reports a lean, or asks a
question that costs less than a wasted afternoon.
